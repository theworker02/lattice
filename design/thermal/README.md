# Thermal surfaces — CONCEPT

Compare an aluminum frame with electrically insulated graphite spreading. [Panasonic PGS](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/faq/faq0099) lists 700–1950 W/(m·K) in-plane conductivity across its range; this is not through-thickness conductivity or assembly thermal resistance. Include adhesive/contact resistance and electrical insulation. Heat pipes are reserved for larger modules after measured need.

Measure PV, power switch, battery and user-touch surfaces separately. A mirror's solar reflectance says little by itself about thermal-IR emissivity. The software RC nodes are assumed 8 K/W and 20 J/K, with no inter-cell thermal coupling. Do not fit temperatures by adjusting constants without recording experiment data and residual error.
