# Optical zoning — CONCEPT

Use independent mirror, sensor-window, photovoltaic and display zones until combined-stack measurements justify overlap. [Spectral research](../../optical/spectral-response.md) contains source curves and limits. [SolarSkin](../../optical/solarskin.md) defines the simulated budget. Require sample identity, angle, polarization and thickness for every optical claim. Do not put NIR sensing and visible display behind a long-pass window without evaluating their incompatible transmission needs.
