# Geometry — CONCEPT

M0: 80 × 80 × 25.2 mm, 86 mm pitch, 6 mm keyed corner. The array footprint is `(4−1)×86+80 = 338 mm`. CAD and drawings are generated by `python tools/generate-cad.py`. The gross simulated optical area is 6400 mm²; a real framed aperture will be smaller. Conduct a swept-volume study before adding tilt: a 6 mm inter-cell gap is not a collision guarantee. Base thickness, mounting and finger clearance remain open.
