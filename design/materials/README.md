# Preliminary material matrix — CONCEPT

Scores below are engineering hypotheses on a 1 (poor) to 5 (favorable) scale, not test data or supplier ratings. Unknown fields require coupons; no material is selected. Optical coatings and adhesives can dominate the substrate behavior.

| Candidate construction | Scratch | Impact | Fingerprint tolerance | Thermal spreading | Low tooling cost | Risks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coated glass + metal frame | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | Edge damage, mass, lamination stress |
| Metallized polymer + frame | 2 | 4 | 2 | 1 | 4 | Scratches, UV aging, coating adhesion |
| Polished aluminum zoned windows | 3 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 3 | Conductive touch/RF shielding, oxidation, glare |
| Dielectric-coated optical coupon | 3 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | Angle dependence, expense, handling, custom scale-up |

Record future measured scores for UV discoloration, delamination, thermal cycling, chemical resistance, corrosion, cleaning residue and finish yield. Weighting proposal: optics 25%, durability 25%, thermal 20%, manufacture/rework 20%, cost 10%. Do not compute a winner until optical tests and quotes exist.
