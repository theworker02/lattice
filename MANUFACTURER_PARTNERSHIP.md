# Manufacturing partnership note

LATTICE is publishing this repository to invite serious engineering and manufacturing collaboration around the Signal Cube and MIRRORFIELD platform.

## What LATTICE is supplying

LATTICE supplies the product intent, industrial-design direction, system architecture, simulator, proposed Signal Cube core/cassette arrangement, M1 pilot definition, draft protocols, documentation structure, test questions and evidence gates. The repository is intended to make a technical conversation concrete and reviewable.

The project does **not** supply a production-ready product package. It does not include final mechanical CAD, selected and qualified materials, released schematics/PCB layouts, approved component sources, finished firmware, test fixtures, safety certifications, production tooling, yield data, or manufacturing authorization.

## What a manufacturing partner is expected to do

A prospective partner is expected to perform most of the work required to turn this concept into a real product. That work includes:

1. Challenge the architecture and identify the design changes needed for a feasible build.
2. Develop dimensioned six-face enclosure/carrier CAD, scale retention, tolerances, service access and assembly process.
3. Select, prototype and measure optical coatings/windows, substrates, PV integration, adhesives, thermal interfaces and protective finishes.
4. Engineer the electrical system: power protection, PCB architecture, harnesses, connectors, test access and firmware target constraints.
5. Source parts and materials, establish suppliers, quote NRE/tooling/pilot quantities and identify schedule and MOQ constraints.
6. Build material coupons, fixtures, first articles and the proposed 12-unit M1 pilot.
7. Run and document optical, thermal, electrical, mechanical, assembly, reliability and manufacturing tests.
8. Establish cosmetic standards, quality records, nonconformance/rework handling and a repeatable manufacturing test process.
9. Advise on regulatory, safety, EMC, wireless, battery and environmental requirements before any applicable claim or shipment.

LATTICE is seeking a partner who wants to help shape the product—not a supplier expected to manufacture an unreviewed image unchanged.

Read the [M1 Pilot Program](manufacturing/M1-PILOT-PROGRAM.md), then respond through the [supplier RFQ pack](manufacturing/suppliers/RFQ-SC-M1.md). No document in this repository authorizes a purchase order, production tooling, fabrication, certification claim, public deployment or customer sale. Any partnership would need its own written scope, ownership, confidentiality, cost, schedule and quality agreement.
