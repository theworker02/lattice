# Pilot operations

**Status: CONCEPT.** This folder covers how an engineering pilot is run safely and repeatably once physical units exist.

## Demonstration checklist

1. Identify unit serial, optical variant, cassette map and firmware revision.
2. Inspect scales, seams, band apertures, cable/fixture condition and any prior open issue.
3. Connect only the approved constrained supply and fixture/host path.
4. Confirm self-test, health and current calibration revision before presenting readings.
5. Record source geometry and environmental conditions for optical/PV demonstrations.
6. Stop on unexpected heating, intermittent connection, damaged finish, suspect insulation or fault latch.
7. Attach logs/photos to the unit record; do not overwrite prior results.

## Field rule

Pilot units remain supervised engineering hardware. No unattended automation, public safety function or external power source is permitted until the applicable hardware, compliance and quality gates are released.

## Data stewardship

The local-first principle applies to pilot evidence: raw fixture and calibration data stay with the project/unit record. Any shared report must identify whether values are measured, simulated or estimated and must omit secrets or personally identifying operator data.
