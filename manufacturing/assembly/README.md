# Proposed prototype assembly — CONCEPT

1. Inspect empty chassis dimensions and keying; fit dummy cover without powered components.
2. Fit separately tested optical coupons in a reversible fixture; retain sample IDs.
3. Establish insulating thermal interfaces with verified clearance and contact pressure.
4. Integrate only reviewed electronics; verify identity supply and disabled main-power state first.
5. Test branches individually with a constrained bench source, then assemble the array.
6. Record firmware, optical stack, BOM and physical serial revisions with inspection results.

This is a review sequence, not detailed electrical assembly instructions. Adhesive cure, fastening torque, magnet capture and battery assembly await the selected design.
