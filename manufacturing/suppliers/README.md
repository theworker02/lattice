Use RFE-0001 to request optical coupon, CNC/print, coating, PCB/assembly and connector options. Record supplier name, contact authorization, quote date, exact part/process, MOQ, yield assumptions, lead time, tooling ownership, replacement/rework terms and evidence links. Source references in research are not approved suppliers. No quote requests have been sent and no purchases are authorized by this folder.
