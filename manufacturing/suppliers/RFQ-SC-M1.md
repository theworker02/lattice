# RFQ response pack — Signal Cube M1 pilot

**CONCEPT — not a purchase order**

## Requested response

Quote and describe a joint engineering + pilot-build path for 12 Signal Cubes using the [M1 Pilot Program](../M1-PILOT-PROGRAM.md). Cite every assumption, quote validity period, lead-time dependency and exclusion.

| Response area | Supplier must provide |
| --- | --- |
| Process recommendation | Proposed scale/carrier, central-core and finishing processes, with alternatives and known risks |
| Engineering scope | CAD/DFM, optical coupon work, fixture design, assembly documentation and test support separated from manufacturing labor |
| Cost structure | NRE, sample/coupon, tooling, per-unit material, assembly, test, yield contingency, packaging, shipping and change-order treatment; no blended unexplained figure |
| Schedule | Milestones from design input through first article and 12-unit evidence review, with gating dependencies |
| Quality | Incoming inspection, cosmetic standard proposal, traceability, nonconformance and rework process |
| Capabilities | Comparable work samples, optical/finish partners, PCB/harness capability, measurement equipment and pilot capacity |
| IP and files | CAD/native file handling, revision control, confidentiality and ownership assumptions |

## Mandatory technical questions

1. How would you retain, align and replace overlapping reflective scales across six faces?
2. What stack can preserve the desired mirror look while providing a known window or partial transmission for S-L/S-P cassettes?
3. How will each cassette be optically/electrically tested before final face closure?
4. What carrier datums make the visible seams repeatable?
5. How do you route flex into the core while retaining serviceability?
6. Which assembly operation is most likely to damage the finish, and what is the recovery path?
7. Which decision should be made before, rather than after, first-article tooling?

Responses should identify changes needed to make M1 manufacturable. They must not assume that an image, provisional envelope or simulation authorizes final material choice, production tooling or safety certification.
