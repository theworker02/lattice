# DFM review checklist — CONCEPT

Before P1 resolve:

- Chassis and optical cover process, draft/undercuts, edge quality, fixture access and dimensional yield.
- Datum strategy, contact mating stack, polarity keying, strain relief, retention and motion sweep.
- Optical coating/adhesive compatibility, baffles, aperture, thermal-expansion mismatch and service access.
- PCB/optics/thermal-stack assembly order and inspection before closure.
- Independent protection, battery containment, electrical isolation and accessible service shutdown.
- Scratch/contamination during manufacture, protective packaging and cleanability.
- Test coverage, calibration, firmware/serial provisioning and rework restrictions.
- BOM substitutions, supply continuity, tooling cost, pilot yield and non-recurring test-fixture cost.

Exit requires an external review record with owner/date, annotated drawings, accepted risks and closed blockers. Generated M0 STL meshes are not DFM approval.
