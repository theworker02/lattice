# SC-A0 / M1 DFM risk register

**CONCEPT** · Open risks require evidence or a decision; none are accepted by omission.

| Risk | Why it matters | Early control | Gate owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mirror stack blocks sensor/PV | A beautiful opaque finish defeats the functional scale | Coupon R/T/A and angular test through final stack | Optical partner | Open |
| Scale seams vary visibly | The exterior identity depends on repeatable overlap | Carrier datums, cosmetic limit samples and fixture alignment | Mechanical partner | Open |
| Finish damages during assembly | Rework may create unacceptable visual mismatch | Protective films, handling fixture and defined rework path | Manufacturing partner | Open |
| Flex fails or pinches | Surface cassettes lose identity/readings | Bend-radius routing and pre/post-closure continuity fixture | EE/ME | Open |
| PV heat distorts readings | Energy experiment can contaminate thermal/sensor evidence | Separate channel mapping and thermal test points | EE/thermal | Open |
| Band light leaks into sensors | Status visualization falsifies optical data | Physical baffles and active leakage test | Optical/EE | Open |
| Core cannot be serviced | An attractive enclosure becomes disposable | Closure/reopen traveler test | ME | Open |
| Uncontrolled variant drift | A/B/C results cannot be compared | Lot tracking and fixed cassette/core architecture | Program owner | Open |
