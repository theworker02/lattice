# Manufacturer onboarding: a calm first read

You do not need to believe every idea in this repository to evaluate it. The point is to decide whether the Signal Cube is a worthwhile joint engineering program.

## In five minutes

1. Look at the [selected exterior reference](../design/concepts/mirror-scale-cube.png).
2. Read [Start Here](../START_HERE.md) for the plain-language product idea.
3. Read the [manufacturing partnership note](../MANUFACTURER_PARTNERSHIP.md) to see what LATTICE supplies and what a partner is expected to own.
4. Read the [M1 Pilot Program](../manufacturing/M1-PILOT-PROGRAM.md): it is the proposed first engagement, not a production request.

## The questions worth asking first

| Question | Why it matters | Where the current answer lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can the appearance be made as a serviceable shell? | The overlapping scales are the product identity | SC-A0 plan and M1 DFM risk register |
| Can selected scales pass enough light to a sensor/PV element? | An opaque mirror cannot act as a hidden light window | Optical variants, coupon tests and DVP |
| Can the core be tested before cosmetic closure? | A beautiful unit still needs traceable electrical results | M1 traveler and hardware reference |
| What is the smallest credible first build? | It limits cost and risk | 12-unit M1 pilot, external low-voltage, one wired host path |
| What does success mean? | The project needs evidence, not a vague demo | M1 gates G0–G5 and DVP |

## What to send back

A helpful first response does not need a final quotation. It can identify impractical features, suggest a better carrier/finish/assembly process, name needed materials or constraints, estimate engineering phases, and recommend the first coupon or fixture. The [RFQ response pack](../manufacturing/suppliers/RFQ-SC-M1.md) lists the requested details.

## Things not to assume

- The rendering is an appearance reference, not dimensioned CAD.
- The simulator is not a physical performance test.
- No mirror coating, sensor, PV cell, connector or PCB part has been selected.
- The project is not requesting a production order or asking a partner to accept unbounded risk.
- LATTICE expects the partner to challenge and improve the plan.

The intended relationship is collaborative: LATTICE brings a distinctive product direction and a structured evidence plan; the manufacturing partner brings the detailed engineering and realization expertise.
