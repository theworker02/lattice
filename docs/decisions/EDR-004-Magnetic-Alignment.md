# EDR-004 — Magnetic alignment

Status: **CONCEPT** / principle accepted, implementation open.

**Problem:** make cells easy to position and difficult to reverse without unreliable power contacts.

**Alternatives:** magnets, rails/dovetails, mechanical clips, screws, combinations.

**Evidence:** no pull-force, tolerance, wear or contact-resistance testing exists for this geometry.

**Decision:** magnets may assist positioning/retention; separate rated recessed electrical contacts and mechanical keying are mandatory design requirements.

**Tradeoffs:** magnets require captive mounting, polarity management and contamination review. Wall mounting needs independent retention analysis. Do not use magnetic attraction as a current rating or a safety lock.
