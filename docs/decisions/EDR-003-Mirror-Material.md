# EDR-003 — Mirror material

Status: **CONCEPT** / open.

**Problem:** achieve reflective appearance without defeating sensing, collection, durability or thermal behavior.

**Alternatives:** coated glass, metallized polymer, polished metal with separate windows, dielectric optical coupon.

**Evidence:** manufacturer beam-splitter and long-pass data show wavelength/thickness/angle dependence; no finished LATTICE stack has been tested. See optical/spectral-response.md.

**Decision:** use zoned construction as the baseline concept; defer material selection pending matched coupon tests.

**Tradeoffs:** separate zones reduce seamless appearance but make optical performance measurable and service easier. Combined coatings introduce cost, yield and touch/RF constraints. Material scores are hypotheses until M1.
