# EDR-006 — Module geometry

Status: **CONCEPT** / M0 envelope proposed.

**Problem:** provide reviewable geometry without fabricating production readiness.

**Alternatives:** square, hexagonal, rectangular multi-cell, variable pitch.

**Evidence:** generated 80 × 80 × 25.2 mm envelope and 86 mm array pitch; no physical ergonomic trial.

**Decision:** use square cells with a clipped key corner and removable cover for M0. Hexagonal facets remain the identity mark, not mandatory cell shape.

**Tradeoffs:** simple array construction and PCB area, but tilt collision, cover mounting, optical aperture and contact geometry remain unresolved. Envelope CAD omits those interfaces and cannot be sent as a production drawing.
